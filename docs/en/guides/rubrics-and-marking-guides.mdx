---

When an activity is graded with a rubric or a marking guide, Autograder needs to know what to select for each criterion. That cannot be asked in a single field, so it takes one more step after saving the settings.

<Steps>
1. Enable Autograder in the settings of the activity and save.
2. Follow the link that appears in the Autograder section.
3. Fill in the rubric or the marking guide as you would for a student: choose a level for each criterion of a rubric, or a score and a remark for each criterion of a guide.
4. Save.
</Steps>

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Autograder stays disabled until you complete this step. The settings page says so after saving.

## What happens afterwards

Autograder applies that selection to every student it grades in that activity, through the grading method of the activity. The grade that results is the one that the rubric or the guide calculates.

The selection is part of the backup of the activity, so a restored course keeps it.
