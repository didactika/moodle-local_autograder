When the site allows it, teachers can choose never to have grades recorded in their name.

## Allow the choice (administrators)

Go to **Site administration > Plugins > Local plugins > Autograder**, open the **Teachers** tab and enable **Allow teachers to opt out**. While the setting is disabled, existing opt-outs are ignored.

## Choose to opt out (teachers)

<Steps>
1. Open your profile and go to **Preferences**.
2. Select **Autograder preferences**.
3. Choose that grades are never recorded in your name, and save.
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="asset:lg-optout.webp" alt="The Autograder preferences page of a teacher, with the option to opt out" caption="The preference of a teacher." url="/local/autograder/optout.php" />

A teacher who opted out is left out when Autograder chooses [who a grade is attributed to](page:concepts/who-the-grade-is-attributed-to). If that leaves no teacher for a student, the grade goes to the fallback grader, or fails if there is none.
