This task takes about ten minutes. It turns Autograder on for one assignment of a course and shows how to follow what it does.

<Steps>
1. Open the settings of an assignment, a forum or a quiz that has a deadline and a grade, and expand the **Autograder** section.
2. Select **Enable Autograder**.
3. Enter the **grade to assign**: a number, or an item of the scale, depending on how the activity is graded.
4. Set the **time to wait** after the deadline before grading, in days, hours and minutes.
5. Save the settings.
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="asset:lg-activity-form.webp" alt="The Autograder section in the settings of an assignment" caption="The Autograder section, inside the settings of the activity." url="/course/modedit.php" />

Students who have already done the activity are processed on the next run of cron. Each one is graded when the deadline plus the waiting period has passed, unless a teacher grades them first.

## See what was decided

Open the activity menu and select **Autograder report**. For each student the report shows whether they are waiting, were graded, were graded by a teacher or failed, and when, with what grade and in whose name.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-activity.webp" alt="The Autograder report of an assignment, with one row per student and their status, date, grade and grader" caption="The report of an activity, with one row per student." url="/report/autograder/index.php" />

> [!TIP]
> A rubric or a marking guide needs one more step: after saving, follow the link in the Autograder section and fill in the rubric or the guide as you would for a student. See [Rubrics and marking guides](page:guides/rubrics-and-marking-guides).
