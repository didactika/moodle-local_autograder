---
title: Who the grade is attributed to
summary: How Autograder chooses the teacher in whose name each grade is recorded.
order: 20
---

Autograder never records a grade as itself or as a site administrator. It chooses one of the teachers of the student, and the grade appears under that teacher's name in the activity, in the gradebook and in the grade history.

## How the teacher is chosen

<Steps>
1. **Course teachers.** Users with a teaching role assigned in the course and an active enrolment in it. The site setting **Teaching roles** decides whether every role that can grade counts or only the selected roles.
2. **Permission to grade.** Only teachers who can grade the activity and can see the student in it. In an activity with separate groups, a teacher without access to all groups can only grade the students of their own groups.
3. **Group match.** The teacher who shares the most groups with the student is preferred. If no teacher shares a group, a teacher with no group is chosen, and failing that, any remaining teacher. In a course with separate groups and a default grouping, only the groups of that grouping are considered.
4. **Tie-break.** If several teachers are equally suitable, the one with the lowest user ID or the one who accessed the course most recently is chosen, depending on the site setting **Teacher selection**.
</Steps>

Teachers who chose not to have grades recorded in their name are left out, when the site allows that choice. See [Teacher opt-out](page:guides/teacher-opt-out).

## When no teacher qualifies

The grade is attributed to the **fallback grader** of the site. If no fallback grader is set, the student is not graded and the failure is recorded: the student appears as **Failed** in the report, with the reason.

> [!WARNING]
> Unlike the teachers, the fallback grader is not checked against each activity. Choose someone who is allowed to grade wherever Autograder is used.

## Which capability decides who can grade

Autograder does not add a capability to decide who a grade can be attributed to. It uses the grading capability of the activity itself.

| Activity | Capability |
|---|---|
| Assignment | `mod/assign:grade` |
| Forum | `mod/forum:grade` |
| Quiz | `mod/quiz:grade` |
| Any other type | `moodle/grade:edit` |
