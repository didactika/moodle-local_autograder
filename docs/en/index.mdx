Autograder (`local_autograder`) is a Moodle local plugin that grades students automatically. Once a student has completed an activity and its deadline has passed, Autograder waits for a configured period and then assigns the grade that the teacher configured, unless a teacher has already graded the student. The grade is recorded in the name of one of the student's own teachers and goes through the normal grading process of the activity, including rubrics and marking guides.

<Screenshot src="asset:lg-activity-form.webp" alt="The Autograder section in the settings of an assignment, with the switch, the grade to assign and the time to wait" caption="The Autograder section of an activity: whether it is on, the grade to assign and how long to wait." url="/course/modedit.php" />

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Install the [Autograder report](https://github.com/didactika/moodle-report_autograder) (`report_autograder`) as well.** This plugin only does the grading and has no pages of its own for reviewing it. The report is where teachers and administrators see what Autograder decided for each student: who is waiting, who was graded and with what grade, in whose name, and what failed.

> [!NOTE]
> Autograder never changes a grade given by a teacher. A manual grade always takes priority, and a student who has already been graded by hand is never graded again.

## Why it exists

Some activities are assessed on whether students take part rather than on the quality of their work: a forum contribution, a reflection, a practice quiz. Grading each one by hand takes time, and students who did the work may wait weeks for a grade. Autograder assigns these grades on time.

Assignments, forums and quizzes are supported out of the box. Administrators can enable any other activity type that takes the work of students and has a grade that a teacher gives, such as lessons and workshops.

## What it does

<FeatureGrid>
  <Feature icon="tune-rounded" title="Per-activity setup">Enable Autograder, and set its grade and waiting period, in the settings of each activity.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="grade-rounded" title="All grade types">Points, scales, rubrics and marking guides.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="verified-rounded" title="Native grading">Grades are saved through the grading process of the activity, so submission status, feedback and the gradebook update as they would for a manual grade.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="event-rounded" title="Deadline handling">Close dates, cut-off dates, and user and group overrides.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="person-rounded" title="Teacher attribution">Each grade is recorded in the name of the closest teacher of the student who is allowed to grade them.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="notifications-rounded" title="Failure summary">An optional weekly message to teachers listing the students who could not be graded.</Feature>
</FeatureGrid>

Besides, teachers can choose not to have grades recorded in their name when the site allows it, and the plugin supports the Privacy API and the backup and restore of its settings.
